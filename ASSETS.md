# Assets

Every tracked non-code asset (image, font, binary, board file) needs a row here
with its source and licence; redistribution must be allowed. `tools/check_assets.sh`
(run by `./ci.sh`) fails when one is missing.

| path | source | licence |
|---|---|---|
| _none_ | The repo ships no third-party assets. Corpus boards are never committed (AGENTS.md). Text/CSV measurement records in `docs/m7-results/` are our own tool output. | n/a |
